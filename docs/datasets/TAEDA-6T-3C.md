# TAEDA-6T-3C

Initial Open TAEDA campaign: six technology platforms × three processor designs.

Technologies: SKY130, ICsprout55, Nangate45, ASAP7, GT3, GT2N.
Designs: PicoRV32, SERV, Ibex.

Baseline population: 18 experiment slots.

The campaign is intended to separate technology effects, design effects, flow effects and configuration effects through controlled experiment groups.
